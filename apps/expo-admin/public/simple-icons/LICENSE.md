# Simple Icons

Brand icons from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), downloaded from
`https://simpleicons.org/icons/<slug>.svg` and used unaltered (single-colour
paths, drawn in the text colour through a CSS mask).

The SVG files are released under CC0 1.0 Universal by the Simple Icons
contributors: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md

Each logo is a trademark of its owner and is used here only to label a link
to, or a sign-in with, that service, as its brand guidelines allow.
